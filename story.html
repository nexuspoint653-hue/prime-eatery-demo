01 — How it started

Nowhere to eat
after midnight.

Dearborn has no shortage of great food, until the clock passes midnight. Prime started with a simple idea: a real, made-to-order meal for the people still out late. Students after a study night, nurses off a shift, families after a wedding, friends who just aren't ready to go home.

So we built a menu around the food people actually crave at that hour. Burgers stacked loud, crispy and grilled chicken, rice bowls big enough to share and shakes with the toppings piled on top. Then we kept the grill on until 2 AM during the week and 3 AM on weekends.

"Fresh patties, made fresh to order. That was the rule on day one and it still is."
