01Who we are

This website is operated by Prime Eatery Burgers & Sandwiches ("Prime Eatery", "we", "us"), a restaurant located at 13530 Michigan Ave #120, Dearborn, MI 48126. When this site says "Prime" or "Prime Eatery", it means this one location.

02Halal kitchen

Our meats are halal and our kitchen is run halal, from the fryer oil up. We do not serve pork or alcohol. If you have questions about a specific supplier or certification, ask us at the counter or by phone and we will tell you what we use.

03Allergens and dietary needs

Our kitchen handles wheat, milk, eggs, soy, sesame and other common allergens, and our fryers are shared. Menu descriptions list main ingredients, not every component of every sauce. We cannot guarantee any item is free of a given allergen. If you have a food allergy, tell us before you order and call us if you are unsure.

04Menu, prices and photos

Prices on this site match our delivery-app listings and may differ from in-store prices. Menu items, prices, hours and specials can change without notice. Food photos show how an item is usually served; portions and presentation can vary from order to order.

05Ordering through third parties

Online orders are placed through third-party services such as DoorDash and Uber Eats. Those orders, payments, fees, delivery times and refunds are handled under that service's own terms and privacy policy. For a problem with an order, contact the service first, then call us and we will help where we can.

06Catering estimates

Catering prices, the instant estimate tool and the event planner on this site give estimates only. Your final price is confirmed in writing after we review guest count, date, menu, location and service. A deposit may be required to hold a date. Michigan sales tax applies.

07Hours

Hours shown on this site are calculated in Eastern Time and are our regular hours. Holidays, weather and private events can change them. Check our Instagram or call ahead if you are making a special trip.

08Trademarks

The Prime Eatery name, logo, photos and written content on this site belong to Prime Eatery. Third-party names and logos, such as DoorDash, Uber Eats, Google and Instagram, belong to their owners and are used only to show where you can find us.

09Accessibility

We want this site to work for everyone. If something on it is hard to use with a screen reader, keyboard or other assistive technology, tell us and we will fix it or help you order another way.
