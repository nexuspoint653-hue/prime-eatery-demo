Privacy policy

What we collect when you use this site, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Last updated October 31, 2026

01Summary

We collect very little. This site has no accounts and does not sell your information. The only personal details we receive are the ones you choose to send us, such as your phone number for deals or the details of a catering request.

02Information you give us

  • Text deals sign-up. Your mobile number and, if you choose, your favorite item.
  • Catering and event requests. Event type, guest count, date, service style, food preferences, your name and phone number. The event planner prepares a text message on your device; nothing is sent until you press send in your own messaging app.
  • Calls, texts and email. Whatever you share when you contact us directly.

03Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes standard technical data, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, to deliver and secure the site. Links to DoorDash include campaign tags so we can see which part of the site an order came from. We do not run advertising trackers on this site.

04Cookies and local storage

This site does not set its own advertising or analytics cookies. Embedded services you choose to open, such as Google Maps, DoorDash, Uber Eats or Instagram, may set their own cookies under their own policies.

05How we use information

  • To answer your question or quote your event.
  • To send the text deals you asked for.
  • To keep the site running, secure and working properly.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

06Text messages

If you sign up for text deals, you agree to receive recurring automated marketing texts from Prime Eatery at the number you provide. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies, usually no more than a few a month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel at any time and HELP for help. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

07Third-party services

When you leave this site to order, get directions or view our social media, the other service's privacy policy applies. That includes DoorDash, Uber Eats, Google (Maps and reviews), Instagram (Meta) and your phone carrier.

08Retention and security

We keep catering requests and contact details only as long as we need them to serve you and meet our legal obligations, then delete them. We use reasonable safeguards, but no website or transmission is completely secure.

09Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP or contacting us below.

11Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be noted on this page.
