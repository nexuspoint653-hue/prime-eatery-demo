Social media disclaimer

How we run our social accounts, what we do with posts that tag us, and what to watch out for.

Last updated October 31, 2026

01Our official accounts

Our official account is @primeeatery on Instagram, plus our Google Business Profile and our listings on DoorDash and Uber Eats. Any other account claiming to be Prime Eatery is not us. We will never message you asking for passwords, card numbers or payment to claim a prize.

02Tagging and reposts

When you tag @primeeatery or use our location, you give us permission to like, comment on and repost that content on our channels and on this website, with credit to your handle. If you would like a repost removed, message us and we will take it down.

03What our posts show

Photos and videos show our food as we make it on a given day. Portions, toppings and presentation can vary. Prices, specials and hours in older posts may no longer be current; this website and our ordering pages show current details.

04Giveaways and promotions

Any giveaway we run on social media is run by Prime Eatery alone. It is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Instagram, Meta, TikTok or any other platform. Each giveaway has its own rules, posted with it. No purchase is necessary to enter unless the rules say otherwise and the law allows it.

05Comments and messages

We welcome honest feedback. We may hide or remove comments that are abusive, hateful, spam, off-topic, or that share someone else's private information. Please do not share personal or payment details in comments or direct messages.

06Creators and partners

When we work with creators or partners and provide free food, payment or anything else of value, we ask them to disclose it clearly, as required by the Federal Trade Commission's endorsement guides. Reviews are the personal opinions of the people who post them.

07Health and allergens

Social media posts are not a complete ingredient or allergen list. If you have an allergy, ask us directly before ordering.

08Third-party platforms

Social platforms are run by other companies under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not control how they collect or use your information.
